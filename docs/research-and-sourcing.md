# Research And Sourcing

This repo treats sourcing as part of the production pipeline.

## Preferred source order

1. `nba.com` official previews, recaps, roster pages, player pages
2. official NBA injury report PDFs
3. team game notes PDFs
4. public X posts for buzz and narrative temperature

## Notes on X

Use X for:

- what the conversation is focusing on
- what team or league accounts are amplifying
- recency and sentiment color

Do not use X alone for:

- injury truth
- lineup certainty
- official schedule details
- standings or seeding claims

## Local storage pattern

Every matchup data module should keep:

- facts used on-screen
- short source labels for cards
- raw source links for auditability

This lets future edits stay fast while preserving traceability.
